# AI-Assisted Healthcare System

A conceptual generative AI-assisted decision-support system designed for NHS-style healthcare environments, with a focus on reducing administrative workload while maintaining clinician oversight and accountability.

## Project Overview

This project explores how emerging technologies could support healthcare organisations, with a particular focus on the use of Generative AI to reduce administrative workload for clinicians.

Several technologies were evaluated, including Generative AI, cloud-native computing, Internet of Things (IoT), cybersecurity technologies, and Robotic Process Automation (RPA). Generative AI was selected as the most suitable technology for the proposed use case.

The resulting conceptual system combines AI-assisted clinical documentation, information retrieval, workflow automation, EHR integration, and human-in-the-loop verification.

## Proposed System

The system is designed around a clinician-centred workflow in which AI supports, rather than replaces, clinical decision-making.

Key capabilities include:

- AI-assisted clinical documentation and summarisation
- Electronic Health Record (EHR) integration
- Information retrieval from clinical knowledge sources
- Workflow and referral automation
- Triage and decision-support assistance
- Clinician review and approval of AI-generated outputs
- Role-based access control and audit logging
- Security, governance, and regulatory safeguards

## Human-in-the-Loop Design

A central principle of the proposed architecture is that AI-generated outputs should not automatically become part of a patient's clinical record.

Clinicians remain responsible for reviewing, editing, approving, or rejecting AI-generated information before it is written back to the EHR. This provides an additional safeguard against hallucinations, inaccurate information, and inappropriate recommendations.

## Responsible AI & Governance

The project considers several challenges associated with introducing Generative AI into healthcare, including:

- AI bias and inaccurate outputs
- Patient privacy and data protection
- GDPR and healthcare governance requirements
- Cybersecurity and unauthorised access
- Professional and organisational accountability
- Staff training and user trust
- Continuous monitoring as AI systems evolve

Proposed safeguards include encryption, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, audit logging, human verification, security monitoring, and ongoing AI evaluation.

## Future Development

Potential improvements to the proposed system include:

- Explainable AI capabilities
- Greater interoperability between EHRs, laboratories, pharmacies, and healthcare providers
- Predictive analytics
- Stronger cybersecurity monitoring
- Continuous model and system evaluation
- Expanded staff training and governance processes
